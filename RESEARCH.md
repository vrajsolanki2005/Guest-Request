GuestRequest — Product Research
GuestRequest is a focused hotel operations SaaS that makes sure every guest request is assigned to someone, tracked against a deadline, escalated when delayed, and resolved with an auditable history.

1. Problem
- Hospitality research consistently treats service failure and recovery as an important area of study. A 2023 integrative review analyzed 263 hospitality and tourism service-failure/service-recovery articles published between 2001 and 2021, showing the depth of research around failures and recovery in hospitality.
- A typical guest request passes through multiple handoffs—from reception down to front-line staff—creating severe tracking and visibility gaps when managed via calls, messages, or notes. Without real-time accountability, hotels struggle to monitor ownership, response times, SLA breaches, and resolution histories. Consequently, hotel requests require active operational ownership and time-based accountability, not merely static storage.

####
2. Research Findings
Finding 1: Service recovery is an established domain.
Extensive hospitality research on service failures, complaint handling, and recovery outcomes confirms that request resolution is a core operational function rather than a simple UI workflow.

Product implication: GuestRequest must track and measure the full post-reception lifecycle.

Finding 2: Response time drives guest retention.
Studies consistently show that shorter recovery times directly increase guest satisfaction and repurchase intent.

Product implication: Time metrics must be prominent—tracking creation, elapsed time, time remaining, at-risk status, and SLA breaches.

Finding 3: Market platforms validate category demand.
Established solutions (e.g DineQube) already center on request queues, routing, SLA tracking, and escalation, confirming proven market demand for operational accountability.

Product implication: The problem space is market-validated; focus should be on superior operational execution.


###
3. Opportunity
Rather than building another full-suite hotel management system, the strategic focus should be a targeted operational control system for guest requests. The MVP must deliver immediate visibility by answering five core operational questions:

Open Requests: What is currently active?

Ownership: Who is responsible for each item?

Wait Times: How long has each request been pending?

SLA Risk: Which requests are at risk or already breached?

Resolution Audit: What has been completed?
